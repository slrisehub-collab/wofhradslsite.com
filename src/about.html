About WOFHRAD-SL

The Women's Forum for Human Rights & Democracy is a Sierra Leonean non-governmental organisation founded by young women, working so that women lead change in their communities and in public life.

Our Mission

Our mission

We work to promote and protect human rights, justice, democracy and good governance in communities, with women in the lead, to bring change and development to society.

Our Story

Founded by young women in Makeni

WOFHRAD-SL was established in 2006 by a group of young women in Makeni, the main town of Sierra Leone's Northern Region. The war had ended in 2002, but abuse of women remained widespread, especially in rural areas, where custom and tradition in a male-dominated society kept women from justice.

The founders were students at the University of Makeni, inspired by a Vice Chancellor who told them the university was "not grooming job seekers but job creators". Our founder, Isha Kanu-Moriba, was working with a United Nations mission and had seen in rural communities how often women were denied their basic rights.

From the start, WOFHRAD-SL set out to promote and protect women's human rights, girls' education, women's advancement and the participation of women and young people in politics, and to promote democratic principles and good governance.

Milestones

Twenty years of work

  1. 2006

    WOFHRAD-SL is founded

    A group of young women in Makeni set up the organisation and register it with the Ministry of Social Welfare, Gender and Children's Affairs.

  2. Early years

    Justice, truth and peaceful elections

    We teach pupils in 10 Makeni secondary schools about the Truth and Reconciliation Commission report, help survivors of sexual and gender-based violence in Safroko Limba reach the police and courts, and run peace training for young people in all 13 chiefdoms of Bombali during a national election. Read more

  3. 2013

    Women's right to land

    With ActionAid, we train women farmers, help six women farmers' associations register, and hold public lectures on women's right to own land. Our first VSO ICS volunteers join the team. Read more

  4. 2014–15

    Responding to Ebola

    We deliver food to 800 sponsored children in two Bombali chiefdoms and support community awareness on preventing Ebola. Read more

  5. 2021

    The Girls Circle Collective begins

    Led by Purposeful, the programme reaches out-of-school girls. WOFHRAD-SL runs 90 safe spaces across 8 chiefdoms in Bombali. About the programme

  6. 2023

    Women candidates trained in six districts

    Ahead of the general elections, we run leadership training for women candidates in Karene, Pujehun, Bombali, Kono, Western Rural and Western Urban. Women go on to win 30.4% of Parliament's elected seats. Read more

Our Values

What guides our work

Inclusivity & diversity

We give equal opportunity to all girls and women, whatever their ability, age, ethnic background, religion, political affiliation or sexual orientation.

Consultation

We believe in good communication. We actively seek and take account of our members' views before making decisions.

Proactivity

Our success depends on setting our own goals and taking steps to reach them, rather than simply reacting to events.

Accountability

We honour our commitments to partners, donors, the people we serve and other stakeholders. We take ownership of our work and correct mistakes promptly.

Integrity

We value honesty, truth, reliability and respect.

Transparency

We value openness and clarity. Our reports and accounts are published on this website.
