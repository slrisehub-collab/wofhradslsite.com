Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the WOFHRAD-SL website collects, why, and what you can do about it. WOFHRAD-SL ({{address}}) is responsible for this information. Contact us at {{email}} with any question about it.

What we collect and why

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you choose, and your phone number, subject and message if you give them. We use them to reply to you.
  • Donation pledges. Your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and the amount, currency and frequency you pledge. We use them to send you payment details and to thank you. No card or bank details are entered on this website.
  • Newsletter. Your email address and the dates you subscribed and confirmed. We use it only to send you the WOFHRAD-SL newsletter. We ask you to confirm by email first, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
  • Security records. To stop the forms being abused, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address for up to one day. It cannot easily be turned back into the address.

We do not sell or share your information for marketing, and we do not use advertising cookies.

Saved pages on your device. To make the site work on slow or interrupted connections, your browser keeps a copy of pages and photos you have already viewed. This copy stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser's data for this site.

WhatsApp. If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.

Who processes it for us

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores form messages, pledges and newsletter subscribers.
  • Resend sends our newsletters, confirmation emails and message alerts to our team.

These services may store data outside Sierra Leone. We use them only to run this website.

How long we keep it

Newsletter subscribers are kept until they unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. Messages and pledges are kept for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and our team deletes old messages regularly.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing {{email}}. You can leave the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.

Changes

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this page and the date above.
