Giving FAQs

How do I donate?

If payment options are shown above, you can use them straight away. Otherwise, choose an amount, add your name and email, and press "Send my pledge". Our team will contact you with payment details. You can also email or call us directly.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Select "Monthly" and tell us the amount. Our team will explain how to set up a regular gift.

Can I give in a currency other than Leones?

You can pledge in Leones, US dollars, pounds sterling or euros. Our team will confirm how to send it.

How do I know my gift is used well?

Our annual reports and accounts are published on our Reports & Accountability page.

I can't give money. How else can I help?

You can partner with us, apply for an internship or volunteer, or share our stories. See Get Involved.
