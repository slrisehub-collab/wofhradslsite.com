Conflict Mitigation & Peacebuilding

Promoting peaceful co-existence in Bombali, Karene, Kono, Pujehun and the Western Area.

Why we do conflict mitigation

  • To promote peaceful co-existence between stakeholders and political party supporters in our six operational districts.
  • To promote measures that reduce conflict in those districts.
  • To strengthen peace ambassadors so they can spread messages of peace to the public.

Where we work

Our peacebuilding work covers six districts across Sierra Leone: Bombali and Karene in the north, Kono in the east, Pujehun in the south, and the Western Area, in both Freetown (Western Urban) and Waterloo (Western Rural).
