Women's Empowerment (ICSP)

Strengthening women for equal and effective participation in leadership and decision-making.

About the programme

Through the Irish Civil Society Programme (ICSP), WOFHRAD-SL works with women's networks in Bombali District to advance women's rights in every sphere of life. That includes women's place in formal and informal decision-making in the district, stronger women's networks, more women in decision-making structures, and holding duty-bearers to account for services that meet women's needs and aspirations.

We strengthen the women's networks through training in advocacy and leadership, and by building links between networks and with service providers across the district. We also work with stakeholders to raise awareness of Sierra Leone's gender laws and to press for their full implementation and enforcement at every level.

The Bombali District Women Leadership Network

The Bombali District Women Leadership Network (BODWILN) is a registered, membership-based network of women from all walks of life in Bombali, who share responsibility for improving the well-being of women and girls in the district. Its General Assembly is open to any woman from Bombali District who supports its mandate, whatever her political party.

How we empower women

  • We identify, mentor and coach women who want to lead.
  • We advocate for women's and girls' participation in governance and decision-making.
  • We promote the rights of women, including women with disabilities, across Sierra Leone and especially in Bombali District.

The national picture

The Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act 2022 was a major step forward for women in Sierra Leone, setting a 30% quota for women in Parliament and local councils. In the June 2023 elections, women won 41 of Parliament's 135 elected seats (30.4%), up from 14.5% after the 2018 elections, according to the Inter-Parliamentary Union.
