Gender Equality & Social Accountability

Supporting gender equality and social accountability through a stronger civil society.

What the project aims to do

The project's overall objective is a civil society strong enough to drive women's empowerment and support women's active participation in democratic governance and development in Sierra Leone.

Its specific objective is to strengthen women's voice, influence and participation in democratic governance, through a stronger civil society.

Results so far

9

How we work

  • Dialogue between citizens and duty-bearers. Town hall meetings bring people and officials together to discuss policies for better health, education and social protection services, and women's empowerment.
  • Gender-sensitive advocacy. Messages suited to each age group promote peaceful elections, women's political participation and women's appointment to positions in governance and leadership.
  • Communities of Practice. District-level groups for women's empowerment act as solidarity networks, coordinating and campaigning for safe spaces and for women's active part in governance and leadership.
  • Networks of Young Women in Governance. The networks champion young women as decision-makers in their communities.
  • Stronger civil society organisations. Capacity building and accompaniment help partner organisations deliver project milestones more efficiently and effectively.
