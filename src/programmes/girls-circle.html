Girls Circle Collective

Safe spaces where out-of-school girls learn, share and grow, so no girl is left behind.

About the programme

The Girls Circle Collective (GCC) works with adolescent girls who are out of school, so that they are not left behind but are empowered to shape their own lives.

The programme began in 2021. It is led by Purposeful and delivered by five partners in different districts: WOFHRAD-SL in Bombali, CARL in Western Rural, MUNAFA in Karene, the Dendembeh Federation in Falaba, and Pekin to Pekin in Moyamba and Bonthe.

Safe spaces

Girls meet with a mentor from their own community in a safe space, where they listen to stories, share their experiences and learn from each other. The sessions cover life skills, livelihood skills, safeguarding, literacy and numeracy, arts and crafts, and poetry.

How we follow up

Girls receive micro-grants to start small businesses and build their futures, and WOFHRAD-SL staff monitor how the grants are used. We also check that mentors have taught the topics they were given, including forced marriage, rape and menstrual hygiene, and that they send their reports through the KoboToolbox data system.
