Peacebuilding

People to People Project

Bringing people and communities together to prevent politically motivated violence.

What the project aims to do

  • Help prevent and reduce politically motivated violence through people-to-people peacebuilding.
  • Give women and young people a stronger voice and leadership as peacebuilders, so they help strengthen the "peace infrastructure" at local and national level.

Our approach

  • People to people. We bring together individuals and communities in conflict to agree face-to-face on ways to keep the peace.
  • Planting the Seeds of Peace. Using Trócaire's methodology, we put young people aged 20 to 35 at the centre of peacebuilding, training them to lead in spreading peace and supporting social cohesion so that peace lasts in their communities.
  • Stronger local organisations. We build the capacity of local civil society partners and networks.
  • Women and youth empowerment. We build confidence, self-esteem and awareness of rights.
  • Inclusive local government. We help local authorities support inclusive governance.
  • Financial inclusion. We support Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLAs).
