Our Mission

Promoting Justice & Inclusive Governance

Founded in 2006, WOFHRAD-SL emerged in the aftermath of Sierra Leone's civil war to address widespread abuses facing women, especially in rural communities where culture and tradition in a male-dominated society blocked access to justice.

We work for the promotion and protection of human rights and justice, democracy and good governance, with women leading change.

Our Impact

Changing Lives, Changing Policies

For 20 years we have worked in rural communities and with decision-makers so that women and girls are safe, heard and represented.

The national picture. In the June 2023 elections, women won 41 of Parliament's 135 elected seats (30.4%), up from 14.5% after the 2018 elections. The Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act 2022 sets a 30% quota for women. WOFHRAD-SL is one of many women's organisations that have pushed for this change.

Source: Inter-Parliamentary Union, July 2023.

The Context

Challenges Women Face in Sierra Leone

Many women still face marginalisation and discrimination in education, employment, political participation and access to justice. Early marriage and a lack of land rights make unequal opportunity worse.

Political Exclusion

Many men still feel women should not hold leadership roles, which keeps women under-represented in decision-making.

Literacy & Education Gaps

Low literacy and a lack of quality education limit women's political and civic participation.

Gender-Based Violence

Violence during elections is used to silence women and discourage them from standing as candidates.
