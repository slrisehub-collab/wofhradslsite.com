Team

Add a staff member with their name and email, and tick Send invitation. They get an email with a link that shows them their name and email to confirm, and lets them choose a password. If email isn't set up, you'll get a link to send them by WhatsApp instead. When someone leaves, press Remove: their account stops working straight away and their profile disappears from the website. To hide someone temporarily (for example during leave), untick Show on website instead.
