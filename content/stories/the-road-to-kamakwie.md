Our recent fieldwork took us to Kamakwie. Calling the route a road may be generous. Parts of it are little more than rocks, streams and rivers run straight across it, the bridges are made of rotten planks, and its potholes have broken down many a vehicle.

Thankfully, the scenery is stunning, so there is something beautiful to look at while you wait two hours for the vehicle to be fixed. It is a bumpy, dangerous and troublesome road, but a beautiful one too. These photos show the road, and us broken down on it.
