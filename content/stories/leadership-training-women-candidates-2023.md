In June 2023, in the run-up to Sierra Leone's general elections, WOFHRAD-SL ran leadership training for women standing as candidates at national and district level. Two-day workshops took place between 1 and 16 June in six districts: Karene, Pujehun, Bombali, Kono, Western Rural and Western Urban.

The training was part of the project "Localization and Working Together for Peace: Repositioning Women-Focused CSOs for Sustainable Peace in Sierra Leone", supported by the United Nations Peacebuilding Fund through World Vision. WOFHRAD-SL worked alongside UN Women, UNDP, World Vision and the Government of Sierra Leone to deliver it.

The workshops brought women politicians from different regions together to share experiences, exchange ideas and plan how to build a peaceful Sierra Leone. They also helped women-focused civil society organisations get better at supporting women in politics and at promoting women's part in decisions at every level.

## Why train women candidates?

Speaking in Karene, WOFHRAD-SL Programme Coordinator Emilia Kamara Jengo set out five reasons the training matters:

- **Empowerment.** Training gives women the skills and knowledge they need to run for office and take a full part in politics.
- **Networking.** Candidates meet other women leaders and potential supporters, which helps them build a base for their campaigns.
- **Skills.** Many candidates need specific campaign skills, such as fundraising, public speaking and working with the media, and the confidence to use them.
- **Representation.** More women in politics means women's issues and perspectives are better represented in government.
- **Breaking stereotypes.** Training and promoting women candidates challenges the biases that have long limited women's political participation.

## What participants said

Feedback from participants centred on three things: more confidence in their abilities as candidates, a better grasp of campaign strategy and tactics, and a stronger sense of community with fellow women candidates.
