Two new VSO ICS volunteers arrived at WOFHRAD-SL on 24 September 2013: Salifu, from Makeni, and Sarah, from the UK.

Both are excited to help deliver our ActionAid Sierra Leone-funded projects, which include raising awareness of the importance of education, training 20 rural women farmers, and building awareness of women's right to land. They are also planning their own programme of school visits.
