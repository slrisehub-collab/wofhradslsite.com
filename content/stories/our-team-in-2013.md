In 2013, WOFHRAD-SL had six members of staff. This is how we introduced them at the time.

## Isha Kanu-Moriba, National Coordinator and Founder

Isha founded WOFHRAD-SL and has long been determined to have a positive influence on young women and men, especially in the north of Sierra Leone. Having grown up and worked in the north, she has always encouraged young people to become activists for human rights, good governance, democracy, justice, equality and peace. She graduated from the University of Makeni (UNIMAK) in 2011 with a BA in Human Resource Management and, at the time, was completing a Master's degree in Human Rights and Conflict Management at the Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna in Pisa, Italy. She built her knowledge of human rights and the rule of law working with United Nations missions in Sierra Leone.

## Emilia Kamara, Acting National Coordinator and Co-Founder

Emilia was born and raised in Makeni. She graduated from the University of Makeni (Fatima Institute) in 2007 with a Diploma in Development Communication, then gained a certificate in Electoral Administration and Civic Education. At the time she was the Northern Regional focal person for the Human Rights Committee, chair of the Bombali Human Rights Committee, chair of the Bombali District Budget Advocacy Network and a board member of St Andrew's Junior Secondary School, Teko Barracks, Makeni. She co-founded WOFHRAD-SL to help create a culture of human rights, good governance and peace in Sierra Leone.

## Francis Gombay, Finance and Admin Officer

Francis is from Bo, in the south of Sierra Leone. He holds a BA in Philosophy and Humanity and a Diploma in Education and Philosophy from the University of Makeni. He joined WOFHRAD-SL to see women's rights championed, to see women stand up for their rights, and to help make sure democratic rules and laws are fully implemented.

## Simeon Kargbo, Field Officer

Simeon is from Port Loko, in the north of Sierra Leone, and graduated from the University of Makeni in February 2013 with a degree in Development Studies. He joined WOFHRAD-SL to gain NGO experience and to see how the organisation's work improves women's empowerment and children's lives. Simeon is now our Head of Finance and Admin.

## Abu Bakarr Conteh, Assistant Field Officer

Abu Bakarr, known to us all as ABC, is from Kamalo, Sanda Loko chiefdom, Bombali District, and attended the Government Secondary School for Boys in Magburaka. He first came to WOFHRAD-SL as a VSO ICS volunteer.

## Andrew Kamara, Field Officer

At the time of writing, Andrew was sitting his law examinations at the University of Makeni.
