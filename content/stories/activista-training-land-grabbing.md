On 5 June 2015, WOFHRAD-SL completed a one-day training for Activistas in Bombali District, Northern Sierra Leone. Participants came from our three operational chiefdoms in the district and from youth groups in Makeni.

The training looked at land grabbing, its effects on communities and how Activistas can campaign and communicate about it. It also aimed to build a shared understanding of how campaigns are planned at local, national and international level, including how to set campaign goals and monitor and evaluate progress.

Participants shared ideas for issues to raise with decision-makers and stakeholders in their communities, and for ways to mobilise people to keep those decision-makers engaged.

The day set out broad directions for strengthening the Activistas' campaigning, campaign planning and related skills.
