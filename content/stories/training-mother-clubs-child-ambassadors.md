Two weeks ago, WOFHRAD-SL visited villages in Bombali District to set up Child Ambassador Clubs, Mother Clubs and School Management Committees. This week we invited two members from each club to Makeni for three days of training: 36 people from six villages in all.

The sessions covered child rights, how to recognise abuse of children, and how to make sure local schools are run transparently. Together they prepared the clubs to support their communities as role models and to promote children's rights, especially the right to education.

The sessions were interactive, and members practised public speaking, negotiation and presentation, so they can confidently share what they learned with the rest of their club and their community. Everyone took part and contributed ideas throughout.
