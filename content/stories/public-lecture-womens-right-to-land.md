On 27 September 2013, 209 people attended a public lecture on women's right to land, facilitated by WOFHRAD-SL with funding from ActionAid Sierra Leone. The lecture took place in Kamakwie, Sella Limba chiefdom, Bombali District.

Its aim was to make sure women in rural communities know they have a right to own property, as set out in Articles 2 and 17 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

The talk was given by respected community figures: the Paramount Chief's representative, the chiefdom speaker, the women's representative (Mammy Queen), the youth chair, the chiefdom imam, a councillor and ActionAid's focal person. Most of those who attended were rural women farmers, and they spoke warmly of the lecture.
