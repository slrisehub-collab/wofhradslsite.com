---
With funding from ActionAid International, WOFHRAD-SL ran a week-long training for 30 community teachers in Bombali District, mainly from Bombali Shebora, Magbaimba Ndowahun and Sella Limba chiefdoms.

The training covered child-centred and teacher-centred teaching techniques, and school management issues such as classroom management, administration, absenteeism and poor attendance. Sessions were led by experienced lecturers from the Northern Polytechnic's teacher training college and the University of Makeni.

Participants and facilitators were pleased with how the training was organised and thanked the organisers and funders. Every participant received a certificate of participation.
