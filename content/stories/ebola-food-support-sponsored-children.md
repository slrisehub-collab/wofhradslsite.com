As part of the response to the Ebola outbreak in Sierra Leone, WOFHRAD-SL distributed food to 800 sponsored children in two chiefdoms of Bombali District, Northern Sierra Leone, with funding from ActionAid International Sierra Leone.

The aim was to stop a health emergency becoming a food crisis. Ebola had made life harder for rural families. Isolated chiefdoms and communities, often restricted from moving around, found it especially difficult to provide for themselves. The food packages included rice, palm oil, salt and Maggi seasoning.

Beyond food, WOFHRAD-SL and ActionAid's Northern Regional Office held stakeholder meetings to prepare for continuing door-to-door and word-of-mouth awareness work. The messages covered the signs and symptoms of Ebola, how to prevent it, how to break the chain of transmission and what to do when someone is suspected of having the disease. The meetings also brought community leaders together to find better ways to stop Ebola spreading in their communities.
