On Tuesday 5 November 2013, WOFHRAD-SL facilitated an accountability forum with Makeni City Council on the theme "Know Your City Development Plan". More than 200 people came to hear the council set out its city plan, the money it receives through taxes and other funding, and how that money is spent.

Fatmata Sieowoh chaired the session. Speakers included Emilia Kamara, WOFHRAD-SL's acting national coordinator; Mr Sandy, regional manager of the Anti-Corruption Commission; Christian Lawrence, governance coordinator at ActionAid Sierra Leone; Ibrahim Fatu Kamara, director of Action for Advocacy and Development in Sierra Leone (AAD-SL); and Numa Maningo, the council's Development Planning Officer.

After the opening speeches, Mr Maningo presented Makeni City Council's development plan, covering the media, water and sanitation, health, education, social and gender affairs, waste management, agriculture and fire prevention.

Participation was high throughout. People who attended said the session was useful and hoped for more like it.
