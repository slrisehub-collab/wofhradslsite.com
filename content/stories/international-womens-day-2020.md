To mark International Women's Day 2020, a group of WOFHRAD-SL's young women joined the Ministry of Social Welfare, Gender and Children's Affairs, women's groups and other civil society organisations for a celebration in Makeni City, Northern Sierra Leone.

International Women's Day draws attention to the social, political and cultural issues women face and calls for women's advancement in all of them. Working with the ministry, WOFHRAD-SL used the day to recognise women who have made a significant contribution to the advancement of women.

The celebration drew many stakeholders, who came to hear the success stories of women in the district.
