In February 2014, four new volunteers joined WOFHRAD-SL through VSO's International Citizen Service (ICS) programme, which pairs young volunteers from Sierra Leone with counterparts from the UK. Here is a little about each of them, in their own words where we can.

## Kadiatu Kargbo

Kadiatu was born and raised in Makeni and holds a diploma in Business Administration and Management from the Northern Polytechnic's Makeni campus. She applied to the programme to learn about people from different backgrounds and cultures, and to build her skills in motivating people, teamwork, communication and public speaking, so she could serve her country and her community.

## Dhakshi Suriar

Dhakshi grew up in Kingston upon Thames in south-west London. She studied Economics and then International Relations, which led to her interest in development. She was delighted to be placed with WOFHRAD-SL because she is particularly passionate about working with organisations that empower women. She hoped to take something of Sierra Leonean culture home with her, and to share some of her own with her counterpart and the national volunteers.

## Charlotte Langridge

Charlotte grew up in south-east England and now lives in Wales. She studied Criminology and Sociology at Cardiff University and International Relations at the University of Bristol. The programme's grassroots and youth development focus appealed to her because, as she put it, effective development "should start from the ground up".

## Emmanuel K. Kamanda

Emmanuel grew up in the north of Sierra Leone, in a family from the east of the country. He studied Human Resources Management at the University of Makeni. His studies gave him a passion for working with young people, which is why he volunteered with VSO ICS: to support young people who need help.
