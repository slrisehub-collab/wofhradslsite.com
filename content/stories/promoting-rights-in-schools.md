With funding from ActionAid International's Bombali Local Rights Programme, WOFHRAD-SL has been promoting rights in schools, raising awareness of the value of education for children, parents, communities and the nation.

On Monday 21 October 2013, we visited Masankorie village in Sella Limba chiefdom, Bombali District. More than 200 people attended, including pupils from the nearby school, teachers, the Paramount Chief and other community stakeholders, and women's groups. On Tuesday 22 October, we gave a talk to pupils and teachers in Pelewala village, Magbaimba Ndowahun chiefdom. A further talk was planned for Manokoh village in Bombali Shebora chiefdom.

Both talks went well. Everyone took part, and all were positive about upholding rights in schools.

## The ten rights in school

- The right to free and compulsory education
- The right to non-discrimination
- The right to adequate infrastructure
- The right to quality, trained teachers
- The right to a safe and non-violent environment
- The right to relevant education
- The right to know your rights
- The right to participate
- The right to transparent and accountable schools
- The right to quality learning
