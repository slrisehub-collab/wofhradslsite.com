On Monday 14 and Tuesday 15 October 2013, WOFHRAD-SL trained 20 rural women farmers in Bombali Shebora chiefdom, with funding from ActionAid. The sessions covered basic agronomic farming methods, seed bank management and post-harvest management.

The aim was to help the women move from subsistence farming to productive farming, so they can earn a profit from their land and give their families better food and health security.

The women took a full part in the discussions and group work. They were full of energy, and in the breaks they even taught our two VSO ICS volunteers to sing and dance. By the end of the two days, every woman could name one thing she had learned that she would pass on to another woman in her community, so that more women benefit and the project's impact lasts.
