WOFHRAD-SL has been lucky to have two volunteers working with us for three months: Abu Bakarr Conteh, from Makeni (known to everyone as ABC), and Sophie Robbins, from the UK. They were placed with us through VSO as part of the International Citizen Service (ICS) programme, which was funded by the UK's Department for International Development and run by several NGOs.

Over the three months, they helped with court monitoring in Magburaka, organised a public lecture and marked essays for a writing competition. Sophie helped edit our reports and concept notes and taught ABC computer skills along the way. Together they created our newsletter and our blog, so we can share our work in Makeni, across Sierra Leone and around the world.

Having the volunteers in the office has been very useful, and we have enjoyed working with them and with VSO. We will miss Sophie when she leaves. ABC has asked to keep volunteering with WOFHRAD-SL after the programme ends, and we are very glad to have him.

We are hoping for two new VSO ICS volunteers when the next group arrives in September. The National Youth Commission (NAYCOM) has also offered us volunteers from within Sierra Leone, through a project run with UNDP.
