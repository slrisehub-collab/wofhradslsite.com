Our Network

Partner Organisations

Trócaire

Ireland

The overseas development agency of the Catholic Church in Ireland, founded in 1973. WOFHRAD-SL's peacebuilding work draws on Trócaire's "Planting the Seeds of Peace" methodology.

WOFHRAD-SL is listed among Trócaire's local implementing partners in Sierra Leone.

Purposeful

Sierra Leone

An Africa-rooted, feminist hub for girls' activism headquartered in Freetown, working so that girls and their allies can access the resources they need to lead change.

ActionAid

Global Federation

An international federation of more than 40 country offices working against poverty and injustice, including a national office in Sierra Leone.

World Vision

Global

An international Christian humanitarian organisation focused on child well-being, community development, and emergency relief.

USAID

United States

The United States' former international development and humanitarian agency.

Its programmes were absorbed into the U.S. Department of State in July 2025.

European Union

EU

Supports development cooperation, human rights, and civil-society programmes across Sierra Leone and the wider region.

DFID

United Kingdom

The United Kingdom's former Department for International Development.

Merged into the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) in 2020.
