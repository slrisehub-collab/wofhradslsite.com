Questions

Giving FAQs

How do I donate?

Choose an amount above and press "Continue to donate". That sends a short email to our team, and we reply with payment details. You can also email or call us directly.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Select "Monthly" and tell us the amount. Our team will explain how to set up a regular gift.

Can I give in a currency other than Leones?

You can pledge in Leones, US dollars or euros. Our team will confirm how to send it.

I can't give money. How else can I help?

You can partner with us, apply for an internship or share our work. See the Get Involved section.
